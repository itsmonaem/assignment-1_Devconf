Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about DEVCONF 2026? Find quick answers below.

What is included in the Pro ticket vs the Standard ticket?

The Standard ($399) ticket gives you access to talks, networking lunches, and 30-day recordings. The Pro ($749) ticket adds unlimited workshop access, speaker meet-and-greets, a VIP networking dinner, lifetime recording access, and 1-on-1 mentorship.

Can I get a refund if my plans change?

Yes. Full refunds are available up to 14 days before the event starts. If you miss the deadline, you can transfer your ticket to a colleague or friend at no extra cost.

How do I get an invoice for my employer?

A formal PDF tax invoice is automatically emailed to you immediately after purchase. If your company requires specific details on the receipt, email our support team.
